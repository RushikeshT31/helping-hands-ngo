About Helping Hands NGO

Helping Hands NGO is dedicated to improving lives through education, healthcare, environmental protection, women empowerment and community development.

Our goal is to create equal opportunities for underprivileged communities and help them build a better future.

Since our establishment, we have positively impacted thousands of families through education programs, healthcare camps, clean water projects, environmental initiatives and social welfare activities.

Our Objectives

📚 Education

Promote quality education and skill development for every child.

♻️ Waste Management

Promoting recycling, cleanliness and sustainable waste disposal.

🏥 Healthcare

Improve healthcare facilities and awareness in rural communities.

🌳 Environment

Protect nature through tree plantation and environmental campaigns.

🩸 Blood Donation

Organizing blood donation drives to support emergency healthcare.

👩 Women Empowerment

Support women through education, training and employment opportunities.

💧 Clean Water

Provide safe drinking water and sanitation facilities to communities.

🤝 Community Development

Build stronger, healthier and self-reliant communities.
